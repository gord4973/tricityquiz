# Legend of Zelda Ultimate Fan Quiz

id `zelda-ultimate-fan`
30 multiple-choice questions for avid Zelda fans. Correct answers fact-checked primarily against Zelda Wiki (zeldawiki.wiki).

1. In Ocarina of Time, who summons Navi to be Link's fairy companion?  
   Answers: The Great Deku Tree / Saria / Kaepora Gaebora / Impa  
   **Correct: The Great Deku Tree**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Navi
2. In Ocarina of Time, Sheik is secretly which character in disguise?  
   Answers: Impa / Nabooru / Princess Zelda / Saria  
   **Correct: Princess Zelda**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Sheik
3. How many years does the Master Sword seal Link away in Ocarina of Time before he awakens as an adult?  
   Answers: 5 years / 7 years / 10 years / 3 years  
   **Correct: 7 years**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Master_Sword
4. Majora's Mask's central time loop lasts how long before the Moon falls?  
   Answers: Three days / Seven days / One day / Five days  
   **Correct: Three days**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Majora%27s_Mask
5. How does Link obtain the Fierce Deity's Mask in Majora's Mask?  
   Answers: Defeat Majora in a rematch / Complete the Anju and Kafei quest only / Buy it from the Curiosity Shop / Give all 20 non-transforming masks to the Moon Children  
   **Correct: Give all 20 non-transforming masks to the Moon Children**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Fierce_Deity_Link
6. In The Wind Waker, pirate captain Tetra is later revealed to be…  
   Answers: Aryll / Princess Zelda / Medli / Jabun  
   **Correct: Princess Zelda**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Tetra
7. What is the true identity of the King of Red Lions in The Wind Waker?  
   Answers: Jabun / Valoo / Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule / Quill  
   **Correct: Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Daphnes_Nohansen_Hyrule
8. In Twilight Princess, Midna is the true ruler of which people?  
   Answers: The Twili / The Sheikah / The Gerudo / The Kokiri  
   **Correct: The Twili**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Midna
9. Who is the spirit that resides in the Goddess Sword / Master Sword in Skyward Sword?  
   Answers: Navi / Tatl / Ciela / Fi  
   **Correct: Fi**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Fi
10. In Skyward Sword, Demise vows that an incarnation of his hatred will forever pursue those who share…  
   Answers: The blood of Din and the spirit of Farore / The blood of the goddess and the spirit of the hero / The Triforce of Power alone / The blood of the Zonai  
   **Correct: The blood of the goddess and the spirit of the hero**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Zelda_Timeline
11. Which Champion pilots Divine Beast Vah Ruta in Breath of the Wild?  
   Answers: Urbosa / Revali / Mipha / Daruk  
   **Correct: Mipha**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Divine_Beast
12. What animal form does Divine Beast Vah Medoh take?  
   Answers: Eagle / Elephant / Camel / Salamander  
   **Correct: Eagle**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Divine_Beast_Vah_Medoh
13. On the Great Plateau in Breath of the Wild, the Old Man is revealed to be the spirit of…

   **Correct: King Rhoam**  
   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/King_Rhoam

   Source: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Rauru

   **Correct: Rauru**
